Notes. APIT is charged on total taxable employment income — salary, cash allowances and other taxable income such as non-cash benefits (vehicle, housing, fuel and similar). The employee EPF contribution of 8% is not deductible for APIT, so tax is computed on the gross figure. Non-cash benefits are taxed but never received in cash, so they are left out of the take-home figure unless you tick “Other income is paid in cash”. Employer EPF (12%) and ETF (3%) are shown for information only — they are not deducted from your salary. Figures are indicative; confirm your liability with the Inland Revenue Department or your payroll department.
